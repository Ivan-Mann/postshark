About Post Shark

Hey everyone, thanks for checking out my website!

My version of social media has no ads, no algorithms, and no endless feed — just photos.

I intentionally used very little styling to make this platform super simple, kinda like a 90s website.

I hope you enjoy using it just as much as I enjoyed building it.
